gameplay.mp4 is World of Warcraft footage. It is Blizzard Entertainment's
material, not mine, and the MIT license in ../LICENSE does not cover it.

It sits here because the front page (index.html) plays it muted behind the
hero. Blizzard's game content policy lets a streamer use gameplay footage
non-commercially; it does not let me pass redistribution rights on to you.

If you fork this overlay, replace this file with a clip of your own.

sounds/*.ogg are voice lines from World of Warcraft's game files, and the same
applies to them: Blizzard Entertainment's material, not covered by the MIT
license. alerts.html plays them on a raid kill, a new best pull, a follow and
a sub (see alertSounds in config.default.js). They are loudness-matched to
-16 LUFS, so they are edited copies, not the original files.
